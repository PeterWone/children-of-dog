# **0. Instructions to the Candidate**

# **1. Micro‑Scene (200 words max)**  
**Prompt:**  
Write a scene where a character enters a space and the room reacts to them.

**Requirements:**  
- No names  
- No backstory  
- No internal monologue  
- Only action, sound, and spatial consequence  
- Must read cinematically  

**Reveals:**  
- Shot discipline  
- Spatial awareness  
- Ability to imply character through behaviour  
- Whether they understand “show, don’t tell” as *action*, not *description*

---

# **2. Consequence Chain (10 lines)**  
**Prompt:**  
Write a chain of ten cause‑and‑effect beats.  
Each line must be a single action that triggers the next.

Example format:  
1. X happens →  
2. which forces Y →  
3. which causes Z…

**Requirements:**  
- No adjectives  
- No emotion words  
- No dialogue  
- Pure causality  

**Reveals:**  
- Systems thinking  
- Whether they can maintain logical escalation  
- Whether they can avoid fluff  
- Whether they understand narrative as machinery

---

# **3. Character Under Constraint (150 words max)**  
**Prompt:**  
Write a moment where a character must make a decision with incomplete information.

**Requirements:**  
- Must include a choice  
- Must include a cost  
- Must include a visible behavioural tell  
- No exposition  
- No explaining the stakes  
- The reader must infer everything from action  

**Reveals:**  
- Decision architecture  
- Ability to encode stakes without stating them  
- Whether they understand character as behaviour, not biography

---

# **4. Dialogue Strip (12 lines max)**  
**Prompt:**  
Two characters argue about something trivial that reveals something important.

**Requirements:**  
- No dialogue tags  
- No description  
- No stage directions  
- Only spoken lines  
- Subtext must carry the weight  

**Reveals:**  
- Ear for voice  
- Ability to encode conflict without melodrama  
- Whether they can write subtext instead of text

---

# **5. Structural Pitch (5 sentences)**  
**Prompt:**  
Pitch a story in five sentences using the following structure:

1. Status quo  
2. Disruption  
3. Escalation  
4. Crisis  
5. Resolution  

**Requirements:**  
- No names  
- No worldbuilding  
- No genre markers  
- Pure structure  

**Reveals:**  
- Whether they understand narrative architecture  
- Whether they can think in beats  
- Whether they can compress without losing clarity

---

# **6. The One‑Sentence Test**  
**Prompt:**  
Describe your writing philosophy in one sentence.

**Requirements:**  
- No metaphors  
- No abstractions  
- No clichés  
- Must be operational  

**Reveals:**  
- Self‑awareness  
- Discipline  
- Whether they can articulate intent without hiding behind language

---

# **7. The Red Flag Check (Yes/No)**  
**Prompt:**  
Answer yes or no to each. No elaboration.

- Do you revise while drafting?  
- Do you outline before writing?  
- Do you write to discover?  
- Do you write to control?  
- Do you prefer character or plot?  
- Do you prefer scenes or sequences?  
- Do you prefer momentum or detail?  

**Reveals:**  
- Process alignment  
- Whether they can follow instructions  
- Whether they try to “game” the test  
- Whether they understand that honesty is the only winning move

---

# **8. Delivery Protocol**

All responses must be delivered in a single document, cleanly formatted, with no commentary, no apologies, no throat‑clearing, no meta‑explanations.
