# Arc 4 Expansion Topics

## Fixed Population Tenet

Forest's community design explicitly rejects population growth after he understands why the valley succeeded. This is a standing policy, with growth handled by spinning up new villages rather than expanding the original.

These people are not rule-bound. They have only one law, which is "Rule none and suffer none to rule." 

Self-limiting population is necessary to support this because that's how you avoid the need all the overheads and capturable nodes of centralisation. It's not that you can't grow "because authority said so". It's because that is the path to _becoming_ the oppressive state. Civilisation itself (creating cities) is a Great Filter. 

You have to make a transition to post-scarcity, post-urban technological society, which is NOT the same as being agrarian.

There is an optimal size: 1.5 * Dunbar's number. This is the largest population in which most of the time most of the people know a given person directly.

## Range Mothers - Spin-Out Village Logic

Range Mothers understand the fixed-population rule and manage an ongoing spin-out process. Each new village is planned around:
- High-priority specialties (what capabilities the overall network needs next)
- Required resources to sustain those specialties
- Minimal dependency on the original valley to avoid backsliding into centralization
