## Usage Notes

### Common Characteristics
- **All models have green blood**
- **Completely silent** - no vocalizations, only movement sounds
- **Smell like cut grass** when damaged or present in numbers
- Some have faint "warm earth after rain" smell when uninjured

### Tactical Patterns
- **Model Ones**: Scouts, flee when outmatched
- **Model Threes**: Hunting packs, triple-hinged jaws, fast
- **Model Fours**: Ambush predators with paranoia-inducing agent
- **Model Fives**: Intelligent coordinators, biological warfare
- **Model Sixes**: Heavy assault, armored tanks
- **Model Sevens**: Parasites (zombie worms) - require anti-Seven medication
- **Model Eights**: Non-combatant biomass carriers
- **Model Nines**: Stealth units with ECM

### Common Combat Scenarios
- Scouts (Ones/Threes) probe defenses
- Main assault (Threes/Fours/Sixes) follows
- Coordinator (Five or higher) directs from behind
- Sevens opportunistically infect wounded/isolated targets
- Eights collect biomass after combat
