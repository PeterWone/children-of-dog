## Executive Summary

This plan adapts the 77-chapter manuscript (~245k words) into ~98 Royal Road chapters averaging 2,500 words each. Key strategy:

1. **Cold Open:** "Apocalypse Now" (Arc 5, Ch 02) as Prologue - hooks readers with urban destruction before pivoting to rural survival story
2. **Strategic splits:** Divide 6 chapters over 6,000 words into 2-3 parts each
3. **Cliffhanger optimization:** End parts at natural tension points
4. **Lulu combat flashback:** New chapter inserted after Ch 19 "Lively" (Arc 4)
5. **MWF schedule:** Starting February 2, 2026 through mid-September 2026
