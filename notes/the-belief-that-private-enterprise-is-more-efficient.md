The civil service puts more effort into placing itself beyond question than in doing its job. The law demands this, and the electorate punishes anything less.

Outsource the work and you lose oversight; corruption follows.

Then oversight is bolted back on. Now you’ve got _two_ bureaucracies with no shared purpose.

Regulation goes berserk because the regulator does not bear the costs.

The user pays more than ever, and government and contractor can blame each other indefinitely.
