Trix systematically draws the scientists into the valley's social fabric through food. Fresh ingredients, shared cooking, informal exchanges of produce — it looks like hospitality but it is, as Autumn observes with approval, deliberate social engineering. She is creating webs of dependency and loyalty that will outlast any formal research contract.

Janna brings vegetables. The Macintyre boys bring beef. Oleksiy and Sissi bring eggs. Meal by meal, the researchers stop being external observers and become community members. They arrive as scientists and leave as neighbours. When Sophia Hachia makes an unannounced visit and watches the social dynamics rather than the research outputs, she says nothing. Her silence is approval.
