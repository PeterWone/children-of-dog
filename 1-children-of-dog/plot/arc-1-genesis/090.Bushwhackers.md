The morning after the first antithesis incursion, Forest and Trix do cleanup work in the citrus grove. Janna's van pulls in — she is alive; the bones that implied otherwise turned out to be from a pig. Forest arms her with Vanguard weapons and invites her to dinner, folding her back into the community without ceremony.

The pair then visit Oleksiy's fortified property. They find him calmly cleaning a rifle on his porch, entirely unrattled; he fought his own private war overnight and won. The contrast between his quiet competence and the chaos of the outside world says everything about what the valley has become.

Forest goes alone to check on Gerry's unoccupied property. The chapter builds a picture of the valley community as a constellation of capable, self-reliant people who quietly look after one another's corners without being asked.
