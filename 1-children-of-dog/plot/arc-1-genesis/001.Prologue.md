# Prologue

A coordinated strike takes out power, water, and transport in all the major cities simultaneously. The attack is precise and overwhelming — roads cut, pipes severed, towers felled, the port destroyed. The reader sees the chaos from ground level through several viewpoints: a police operations centre watching its board go solid red, a water plant supervisor watching pressure drop to zero in four places at once, a cop whose patrol car stops at the edge of a crater where the motorway used to be.

No demands. No explanation. The reader is left burning to know why.
