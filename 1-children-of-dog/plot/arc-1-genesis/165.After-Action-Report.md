Sophia's confidential memo to Director Grancock. Formal in structure, precise in language, it covers the casualties, the security upgrade to a PMC ground team, and the fact that research continuity has been maintained. The professional tone does not quite conceal the writer's respect for what she has witnessed.

The key complication: Autumn distributed all research data globally without authorisation during the crisis. Sophia is furious. She is also honest enough to acknowledge that the morale effect on the surviving researchers was profound and that the data is already saving lives elsewhere. She recommends not investigating the breach. The memo closes with a personal note — "Let them work. Let the Vanguard protect them his way." A private addendum notes she will make formal noise about the data breach and then acquiesce, banking it as leverage for later. It is the most candid thing she has written in years.
