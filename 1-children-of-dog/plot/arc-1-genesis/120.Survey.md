Dr Amanda Singh arrives separately from the main delegation, in her own battered 2008 Landcruiser rather than a government vehicle — a detail that tells Forest everything he needs to know about her. She is an environmental scientist with real field experience, not a desk researcher. Over tea she lays out the true scope of what a permanent research station would require: not a weekend field trip but a serious, sustained operation.

She and Forest and Trix survey the valley together. Amanda identifies Gerry's terraced property as the ideal site. Throughout the survey she treats the local residents as partners and experts rather than obstacles or subjects, integrating herself into the community's social logic almost immediately. She is the kind of researcher the valley can actually work with.
