Kellerman predicted natural selection would eventually find an answer to the valley's defences. It does. A hive on the eastern ridge achieves symbiosis with native fungi — exactly the breakthrough they feared and hoped never to see. During a two-day observation window the hive launches a coordinated dawn ambush using Model Sixes: cow-sized, armoured, and organised. Meldrum, Hendricks, Park, and Martinez are killed. Forest arrives too late.

Sophia arrives to evacuate and shutter the project. She finds the surviving researchers grimly committed to continuing. Forest brokers a compromise: a PMC ground team under Eric Webb will provide professional security while the researchers keep working. Four memorial stones are placed at the station entrance. They become known as the first line of de fence — the joke is not entirely a joke.
