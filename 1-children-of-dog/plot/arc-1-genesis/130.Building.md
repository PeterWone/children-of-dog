Construction of the research station begins. The first trucks bog immediately. Forest and Oleksiy recover the vehicles using improvised anchors, doing calmly in an afternoon what would have taken a city crew a week to organise. Oleksiy insists on proper concrete foundations over the scientists' budget objections; Amanda backs him, understanding that out here you build to last or you rebuild forever.

Dr Kent, the most determinedly academic of the researchers, is given the chance to operate the excavator when a granite boulder needs moving. The machine transforms him. The chapter closes with the group collaboratively designing and building a water tower with passive filtration — elegant low-tech engineering arrived at by people who know how to think, working with people who know how to build.
