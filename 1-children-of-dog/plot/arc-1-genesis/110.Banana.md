Antithesis dogweeds attack the Macintyre property's prize Brahman bull, "Banana." The Mac boys — Bryce and Cameron — hold off the swarm with rifles until Forest arrives and clears it with Vanguard weapons. The crisis isn't over: Banana has been infected by a Model Seven zombie worm.

Forest injects the bull with anti-parasitic medicine. What follows is an agonising few minutes as Banana convulses through the treatment, too stubborn to lie down. He survives through sheer bloody-mindedness. Daniel Macintyre, watching his animal nearly die and then refuse to, thanks Forest with the directness of a man who means it. Forest arms the Mac boys for future incursions. The chapter adds the Macintyres to the valley's informal mutual-aid network and establishes the community's pattern: neighbours helping neighbours.
