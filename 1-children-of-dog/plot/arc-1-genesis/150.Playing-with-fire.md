The first controlled experiment nearly kills Dr Chen when a hive attacks faster than any of the models predicted. Forest saves him with a precisely thrown Flesh Melter, and new safety protocols are enforced without debate. The near-disaster does not stop the work; it calibrates it.

Minor incursions settle into a regular rhythm. Forest hunts them with a rocket launcher he calls the MIL — Mother-in-law — assisted by Autumn and increasingly by the researchers themselves. Dr Morrison begins studying the thermal and quantum signatures of antithesis death. Weapons training becomes part of the community's life. Sophia makes a second visit and observes without comment a civilian-military hybrid that has no name in any official taxonomy.
