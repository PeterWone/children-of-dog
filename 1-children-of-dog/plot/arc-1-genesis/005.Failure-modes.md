# Failure Modes

Thirteen years before the present day. A man fed up with regulatory overreach and corporate extortion sells his house and moves to a remote valley with his partner Trix to escape "the lunatics." For a while it's good — hard work, quiet neighbours, room to breathe.

Then the antithesis arrive at his door. He survives an ambush by a dog-weed only by shoving his arm deeper into its jaws and beating its skull in with a creek stone. Badly wounded and fading, a system prompt appears in his vision — the Vanguard initialisation sequence. The valley he bought to escape the world is about to become the centre of it.
