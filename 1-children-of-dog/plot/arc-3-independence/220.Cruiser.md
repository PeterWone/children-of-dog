Forest explains to a boy why he will not convert his old Landcruiser to hydrogen. The explanation is a full Socratic lesson in thermodynamics: energy density, Boyle's law, the physics of compression, why the mass of a pressure vessel eats the efficiency of the fuel it stores. Autumn materialises as a tweedy professor to assist with the demonstration.

The Landcruiser is the right tool. The chapter is about Forest enjoying himself more than he admits when he teaches, and about a child who is genuinely trying to understand, which is the only kind of student worth having.
