Sophia sends Lulu to a normal school for social interaction, partly because the education department has been making pointed suggestions. Three days of escalating incident reports follow.

Physics: Lulu corrects Mr Clavish's parabolic trajectory demonstration — air resistance, Coriolis, relative velocity — not rudely, just accurately. When she mentions that her personal AI is called Tictac the class erupts. The teacher needs a drink at 10:47 AM. Chemistry: Mrs Walsh demonstrates a mild oxidation reaction; Lulu lights up about thermite, explains it is hard to ignite so sparklers are the reliable shop-bought igniter, and then proposes synthesising perchlorate from diabetic salt via electrolysis, with an ice-cream maker for bulk production. Mrs Walsh reaches for the phone to call the guidance counsellor. And possibly the bomb squad. Mathematics: Lulu suggests simply deriving the quadratic formula from first principles, then projects annotated equations — with Tictac's corrections — as translucent glyphs on the unused part of the blackboard. A printer in the head of department's office starts running. Advanced Physics (emergency substitution): Dr Heidi Jansdottr, a retired university professor, is brought in. She tries special relativity; Lulu corrects the standard "time dilation" framing — it is space that stretches, not time that compresses, and c is a Zeno paradox, not an absolute wall. Jansdottr sits down heavily. On the way home she wonders whether there is a fix for creaking bones, too. Social Studies: Ms Rodriguez tries conflict resolution; Lulu asks what you do when diplomacy fails against something that literally cannot coexist with human life. Then she climbs out a second-floor window, scuttles along the brickwork, retrieves a potter wasp's nest, and gives the class an unscheduled lecture on predator-prey cycles while tweezering out paralysed Huntsmen spiders for the projector. Dangerous? Only if a wasp stings her and she falls four metres, she explains, and her nanites would fix that up.

The staff meeting. Principal Williams reads out the incident list: thermite; orbital mechanics; antithesis combat tactics described with disturbing relish; concealed weapon used to paralyse a bully, who was then wrapped in a "silky white cocoon of unexplained provenance" and hung from the front gate as a Halloween decoration; wall-climbing; wasp nest. He actually has to stop and put his hand over his eyes at the cocoon detail. Sophia points out that Lulu did not bring a weapon — she is a Vanguard and is resupplied in combat, and demonstrates by summoning an expensive box of chocolates from thin air. Mr Park drops his pen. Mrs Walsh makes the laugh-shaped sound she has been suppressing since the thermite.

Dr Jansdottr ends the meeting: "Leave her with me. We like each other's company and I am learning new physics for the first time in thirty years." She gets her gifted students exemption. The education department's representative lasts exactly one conversation with Lulu before recommending "specialised alternative educational arrangements." Sophia never asks what Lulu said to him. Dr Jansdottr seems pleased, bordering on smug. There is chocolate.
