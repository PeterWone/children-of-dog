Leroy Bristow, Margaret's intelligence handler, reads her reports with growing alarm. His colleague David reads alongside him. They both recognise the pattern: she has gone native. The tone of the reports has shifted across weeks from operational assessment to something closer to testimonial. Leroy paraphrases her register with dry accuracy: "Day 63: The trees, so many trees. They call me."

He must now decide whether this is a profiling failure — they sent the wrong person — or whether something genuinely unusual is happening in the valley. The second possibility is more troubling, because if the community can neutralise a trained operative through nothing more than sincerity and competence, the implications are significant.
