Alex — industrial chemist — arrives at Sally's recommendation. Forest greets him with the information that Sally has decided he belongs here, even if he hasn't figured that out yet. Sally explains the flour-replacement project that the valley children started and which now needs serious chemistry. Forest takes Alex for a walk.

The chapter title is a pun: Alex's speciality is seals — industrial gaskets and sealing systems. Forest doesn't share the "Alex the Seal" jukebox joke with him yet. He is storing it for later, which is itself a form of affection.
