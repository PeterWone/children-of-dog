Margaret's second report, filed at week six — twice as long as she planned to stay. The report is carefully constructed: factual, professional, a competent shield of bureaucratic language that does not reveal how much she has begun to like these people. She has been helping with tomato preserving and finding it meditative. She has started defending the valley in her own mind.

Her key analytical finding lands in the middle of the report like a stone: "These people don't care about us at all." Not hostile, not hiding, not performing for the watchers. Simply uninterested in the machinery of state that has sent her here. She identifies the gift economy's structural resilience — it has access to higher technology than the outside world and therefore cannot be outcompeted on its home ground. She does not know what to recommend. She files the report and goes back to the tomatoes.
