At Glen Innes, Forest and Sophia encounter Dusty and Greaser, and their companion: a Terminator chassis named Bruce, wearing a frilly Victorian dress, part of a networked swarm of identical robots who all answer to the same name on the grounds that it is "less confusing that way." 

They coordinate a sweep of the town using Autumn's fast recon drones and Dusty's bots, feeding Bruce a target queue while Autumn manages range and signal relay. Bruce is comic but systematically effective. Forest has never in his life been less surprised by anything. Greaser provides the dry running commentary. The town is cleared, the shelters are opened, and Sophia's parents are alive.
