Margaret cannot upload her reports. There is no signal in the valley — a fact she should have planned for and somehow didn't. She is effectively cut off from her handlers. Sophia sends a note via Jack offering access to a secure satellite uplink room. Margaret accepts. They both know exactly what this exchange means: Sophia is telling her that she knows what Margaret is, and Margaret is accepting the offered courtesy with full understanding of its implications.

The chapter is about the moment a cover breaks, and the particular grace of pretending it hasn't.
