The valley's harvest festival: entirely organic, unplanned, no committees. Music comes from teenagers — Jim Morrison's son doing an acoustic set — and kids with guitars and bongos, and then Priya's family performing classical Indian music on tabla and harmonium. Autumn's presence drone bobs along, in its element.

Margaret is there. By the end of the evening her posture has finally relaxed. She does not appear to notice when it happens. The chapter is the clearest account of what the valley has become: a place where people want to be, and where wanting to be there is enough of a reason to stay.
