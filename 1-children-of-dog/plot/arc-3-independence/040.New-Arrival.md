Margaret Torres arrives at the valley as "administrative liaison" — actually a government intelligence operative, briefed and prepared. Sally sweeps her in with coffee and warmth before she can establish any professional distance, and the cafeteria confounds her immediately: fresh food, no payment system, genuine hospitality with no visible mechanism of exchange. Her training does not cover gift economies.

Sophia arrives in Vanguard armour, delivers a brief welcome, and leaves before Margaret can get through her prepared introduction. Margaret deploys Cover Story Version A and finds it lands without apparent suspicion. She begins her surveillance assessment, but the community is disconcertingly genuine. She writes her first field notes with the careful language of a person documenting something she does not entirely understand.
