Priya and the Patel aquaculture team are running low on supplies that cannot be sourced locally — pH strips, pump seals, the consumables a functioning system eats through. Tom Bradley proposes building the replacement systems from scratch using local materials. The question is whether that is actually possible.

Forest reveals that he has had a large excavation done, already. He takes them to see the beginning of a purpose-built aquaculture facility using local stone, timber, and clay — infrastructure designed from the ground up to be self-maintaining. The chapter turns the supply problem into its own solution.
