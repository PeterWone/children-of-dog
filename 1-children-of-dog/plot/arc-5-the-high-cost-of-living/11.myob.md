Government officials demand action against the "terrorists." Mr Grey, intelligence, explains in a flat voice: they are not terrorists anymore. They won. The rods from god cannot be intercepted, and whoever controls them controls the high ground permanently. The new power's demands are strikingly simple: nothing. All you have to do is mind your own business.

Mr Grey decides to visit his wife's permaculture farm. The decision is practical — she has food, water, skills, and is not in a city — and it is also a reckoning. He has spent his career in a system that is now over, and he is walking away from it with more equanimity than expected. Somewhere, a tired woman hangs old skis on the wall of a longhouse and does not look at the news.
