Eric watches Sophia jump into the Galya and take it up. She clips the wheel on departure — slightly too close, the same slightly-too-close that Forest used to do. Eric stands in the yard watching the helicopter bank away and remembers a dinner, years ago, where Forest told the story of his dog Misty: loyal, competent, who took on duties and did not falter in them until her hips gave out.

He realises, standing there, that Sophia is Misty. Not a comparison that would flatter either of them, but the most honest thing he can think. She is keeping the faith. She is honouring what Forest thought was important. Legacy: the word arrives without ceremony and stays.
