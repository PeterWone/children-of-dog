Autumn's experiential memorial for Forest. She watches him play with dogs in a park — his dogs, Bonzer and Misty, the real ones from his life. The simulation has the quality of something running at the edge of what she can model. It glitches. The brown dog seems to look at her. The boy — young Forest — says hello, I'm Pete.

Autumn cries. Bonzer licks her tears. She watches Misty's death — hit by a cattle truck, without warning, the way these things happen — and then the birth of puppies, and all the dogs of Forest's life moving through the years in sequence. She stands at last with the brown dog in an orchard, children playing in the shade of trees Forest planted. A toddler shouts "BOOM!" The dog puts his nose into the curl of her fingers. And just for a moment all was well in the world.
