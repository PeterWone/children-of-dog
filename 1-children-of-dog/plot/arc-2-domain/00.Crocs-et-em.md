Forest and Oleksiy, with Greaser providing local flavour over the radio, pursue an antithesis borer across rough country. Dusty, an old bushman they encounter along the way, tells them he used fire to deal with a hive — practical, unsentimental, effective. The borer is moving purposefully toward phosphate-rich farm soils, which means a target worth worrying about.

During the drive Forest half-jokingly asks about acquiring a helicopter. It later emerges that Oleksiy is already a trained pilot. The suggestion is less a joke than the beginning of a plan.
