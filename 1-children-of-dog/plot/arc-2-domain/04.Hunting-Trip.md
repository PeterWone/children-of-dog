Forest, Oleksiy, and Autumn chase the borer across outback New South Wales. The creature is navigating toward phosphate-rich farmland with a directness that suggests purpose rather than foraging. Forest, half-joking, suggests buying a helicopter for this kind of pursuit. Autumn immediately starts making enquiries.

Oleksiy is piloting their current vehicle. Forest wanted to fly ther helicopter but Autumn discovers his flight experience is mostly Microsoft Flight Simulator, whereas Oleksiy's dark past includes significant experience as a combat pilot. Autumn quietly arranges a helicopter with characteristics particularly well-suited to a fully trained pilot — she is already ahead of the conversation.
