The aftermath of the borer seed dispersal demands a ground-level response. Sophia leads Eric's team personally into the field to check seventeen sites across New South Wales where seeds may have landed. This is her first fieldwork, and she is upfront about her inexperience while insisting she needs to understand what she asks of her people from the inside.

At a farm near Gundagai, what begins as a seed-clearance exercise escalates when a hive under the tree line produces dogweeds faster than the team can kill them. Sophia, having been given careful instruction but no real experience, finds herself behind a dam berm with her rifle up. She fires, misses, fires again. She orders the abort at the right moment. The chapter shows Sophia deciding to lead from the front — and then doing it, imperfectly and honestly.
