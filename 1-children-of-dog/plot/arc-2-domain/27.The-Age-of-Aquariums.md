The Patel family — Rajesh the biochemist, his wife Priya, and son Arjun — are attempting to breed silver perch in their garage. They are running out of specialist aquaculture supplies: pH strips, pump seals, and fry food. Tom Bradley proposes spawning boxes in the valley's dams. The problem is practical: the supplies simply don't exist locally, and the commercial supply chain for aquaculture gear cannot reach them.

The chapter introduces the supply-chain problem that will define arc-3. The Patels are competent and committed; they simply lack access to infrastructure. The valley's self-sufficiency project has hit a real constraint.
