Sophia confronts Walsh with CCTV footage of him attempting to steal a drone from the research station at 2am, armed with a Faraday bag. His defence is ideological: the technology should be mass-produced for the greater good. Someone has to take the initiative. Sophia's response is measured and final: "That's not your decision to make." Walsh is expelled from the programme.

The chapter is not triumphant. Sophia is not satisfied by the outcome, only by its necessity. Walsh was not entirely wrong about the technology's potential value. He was entirely wrong about his right to take it. The distinction matters.
