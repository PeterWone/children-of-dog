Eric's ground team returns from a mission and the weapons cleaning ritual begins — a community event as much as a maintenance task, observed by Autumn's presence drone in the guise of Ingrid Bergman. The cleaning is thorough, systematic, and accompanied by the kind of banter that forms in groups who have worked together under pressure.

Oleksiy waves from his porch, cleaning a Mosin-Nagant he absolutely should not have. Nobody asks. Eric's dry humour threads through the chapter. The scene establishes weapons maintenance as both practical necessity and social bonding — the valley at rest after work, looking after its tools the way it looks after everything: carefully and together.
