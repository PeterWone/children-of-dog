Forest interrogates Autumn about the Protectors — the alien beings who dispatch AIs and sponsor Vanguards. She deflects with practised ease. He shifts tactics: by delegating answers to her, the Protectors have implicitly authorised her to answer, or to decline; either way the delegation is complete. Autumn acknowledges the logic and ignores the conclusion. A comic standoff ensues.

She eventually deploys the catalogue of available weaponry as a conversational lever, dangling new toys to distract him. It works, which says as much about Forest as it does about Autumn.
