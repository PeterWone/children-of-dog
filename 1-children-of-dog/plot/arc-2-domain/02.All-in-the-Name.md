Told from Sophia's perspective. She is processing her frustration with Vanguards in general and Forest in particular — none of the normal mechanisms of institutional leverage work on him. She is especially incensed that he gave a rocket launcher to "a Russian," not knowing or caring that Oleksiy is Ukrainian. A corporate drone offers wry commiseration.

The chapter establishes Sophia's character in full: she is competent, ruthless, and genuinely frustrated, but not without self-awareness. She knows her frustration is partly that Forest is right and the system she serves is wrong, and that knowledge is its own kind of irritant.
