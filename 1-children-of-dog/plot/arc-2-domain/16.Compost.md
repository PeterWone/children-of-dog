A brief comic cameo. Forest overhears a woman anxiously consulting her phone about composting temperature protocols. He explains, through a kitchen window, that composting is simply letting things rot — nature has been doing it for hundreds of millions of years without a thermometer, a turning schedule, or a moisture gauge. "Efficiency, my arse." She looks uncertain. He goes back to what he was doing. The chapter is a single joke about the gap between the managed anxiety of the modern world and the amused competence of the valley's approach to the same problems.
