A podcast-style interview with Janna about her nursery and the valley's philosophy. She speaks about community, resilience, growing unusual plants, and the way animals understand loyalty in a way that most people forget. The conversation is warm and unhurried, and Janna proves to be a natural teacher — she has been growing things long enough to have actual opinions about it.

Lisbeth, a young child, appears throughout the chapter as a recurring presence, asking questions and accepting answers with the complete seriousness of the very young. The interview captures what the valley has become from the ground level: not a plan or a project, but a way of life that works.
