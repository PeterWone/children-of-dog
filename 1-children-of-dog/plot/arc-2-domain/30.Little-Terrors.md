Scott and Aiden, teenage valley boys, encounter a large dogweed near Old Tom's property while out on their own. They recognise it correctly, assess the threat accurately, and debate calling it in versus handling it themselves. The debate is conducted seriously — they are not reckless.

The chapter's point is in how these teenagers respond: not with panic or bravado but with the calm, methodical assessment of people who have been trained and trusted. The valley's culture has given them competence, and competence has given them composure. They hold their nerve.
