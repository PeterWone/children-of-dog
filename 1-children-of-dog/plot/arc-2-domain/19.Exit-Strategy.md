Walsh's confidential memo to the Advanced Materials Consortium board: a detailed corporate autopsy of the valley project. The analysis is honest and thorough. The valley is deliberately unmonopolisable: research is open, the gift economy creates no assets to acquire, and Autumn watches everything with a patience no corporate surveillance budget can match. Walsh identifies the valley not as an obstacle but as proof that alternative organisational structures can outperform traditional ones on their own terms.

His recommendation is clear: abandon the project. It is culturally incompatible with commercialisation, and attempting to force compatibility will fail. The memo is the most intellectually honest document Walsh has ever written, and he will never send it to anyone who can do anything useful with it.
