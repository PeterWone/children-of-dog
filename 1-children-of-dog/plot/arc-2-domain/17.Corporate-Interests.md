Walsh, from the Advanced Materials Consortium, is losing patience. Three months into the research project and there has been zero technology transfer. The valley does not license, does not sell, and does not appear to understand the concept of intellectual property as a revenue mechanism. Every time Walsh's team attempts to collect samples or data for their own analysis, they find themselves gently redirected or simply lacking the access they assumed they would have.

Autumn blocks sample collection requests pending what she describes as adequate ecological context. Walsh and his colleague Thompson are convinced there is a strategy at work. They are correct, but they cannot prove it, and the polite friendliness of the valley community makes confrontation feel both rude and unproductive.
