Sophia presses Forest on why he is so consistently contrary. He explains his philosophy of trust earned through action rather than conferred by role or position. He chose to trust Sophia and Eric because they demonstrated judgement and integrity, not because their job titles obligated it. The personal note underneath is clear: he values them.

Autumn, materialising in the guise of a KGB officer with impeccable comic timing, reveals that she watches everyone in the valley — including Sophia — comprehensively and without apology. Her reason: "Because he needs you more than he knows." Sophia is unsure whether to be reassured or unsettled. Both, probably.
