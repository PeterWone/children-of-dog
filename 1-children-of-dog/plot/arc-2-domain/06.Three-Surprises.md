Sophia receives a dinner invitation from Forest and Trix — the first real contact between them outside official channels. She arrives to find a table set in a clearing. The first surprise is the hospitality itself: genuine, unhurried, nothing she can leverage or dismiss.

Forest reveals during dinner that he holds freehold title to his land but that the government has never issued the deed. He invokes feudal landholder rights and obligations with complete seriousness. The second and third surprises come as the evening progresses; by its end, Sophia is engaging with Forest as a person rather than a problem. Something has shifted in how she sees the valley and her own role.
