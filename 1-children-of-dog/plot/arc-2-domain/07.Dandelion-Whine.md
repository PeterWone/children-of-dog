The borer's seed pod detonated in the bunker, scattering seeds across a broad swathe of New South Wales. The scale of the dispersal is immediately alarming. Autumn contacts a Canadian dust-monitoring specialist to track the spread. Oleksiy delivers the assessment with characteristic brevity: "Too small to shoot. Don't burn."

Forest and the others work through the implications of a problem that can't be solved by conventional means. The chapter is about the aftermath and the new scale of what they're dealing with. Sophia, tangentially informed, approves enhanced research data security protocols — a sign that she, too, is taking the long view.
