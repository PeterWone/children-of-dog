The borer has retreated underground into an old disused bunker — an abandoned redoubt with a faded Eureka flag still pinned to the wall. Forest sends Autumn's drones in via a bee relay network to map the interior. The chamber is vast and unexpectedly atmospheric, filled with old furniture and the residue of long-ago occupation. The borer has blocked the far exit.

Forest does not want to destroy the redoubt. As they plan the kill from outside, the borer suddenly sprouts a seed pod. The chapter ends on that image — time is now against them.
