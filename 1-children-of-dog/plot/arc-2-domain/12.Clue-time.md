Three vignettes of valley life, each a small education in how the community thinks. Shorty watches the outside news and reaches the conclusion, apparently for the first time, that he has never once had genuine political representation. Sheila tells him he sounds like Forest. He decides this is a compliment.

Ken Murchison, a veteran with rage and nowhere to put it, is reached by his son's uncomplicated excitement about fish nurseries and the prospect of going crayfish hunting together. The chapter catches him at the moment the valley offers him something the outside world never could: a reason to hope rather than despair.

Oleksiy teaches a group of valley boys who accidentally walked across his shooting range. Their question about why they couldn't hear the rifle fire leads him to explain, with evident pleasure, the Vanguard noise-suppression technology and then demonstrate his MIL. The boys are left staring at a crater where a target used to be. Oleksiy switches the space magic back on. "No need to upset ladies."
