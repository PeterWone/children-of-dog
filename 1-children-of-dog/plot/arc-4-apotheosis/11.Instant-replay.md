Lulu asks to borrow Oleksiy's gravity hammer — Forest built it for breaking granite, neutronium in the head, some inertial frame trick that means it weighs half a tonne for everyone except Oleksiy. Tictac hacks the trigger. She heads into the granite hills to try it out, while Bruce stays on the road with the ebikes.

She finds the hills occupied. Twenty contacts visible, probably forty behind them, and an unregistered hive four hundred metres northeast that Tictac declines to mention until the situation has fully developed. She splits — four tactical minds assessing terrain, running numbers, checking gear, marking exits — and goes to work. The hammer is extraordinary. She kills in efficient sequence, gets the head stuck in an impact crater and has to wrestle it out, kills more. When the hive itself starts emptying, she finds a killbox between two granite boulders and makes them come through the gap one at a time. Three hundred and forty-seven confirmed kills. Fractured arm, compromised knee, armor cracked in seven places, field generator dead, hammer handle bent forty degrees and riddled with stress fractures. She walks back down the hill toward the lights and music, hammer over one shoulder, collecting three more Model Ones on the way without breaking stride.

She kicks the longhouse door open — her arms are tired and she is completely out of fucks — shedding armor as she walks in: chest plate, boots, cracked visor, blood-spattered and foam-sealed. She bellows at Oleksiy that she brought his hammer back and holds it up. He looks at the hammer. Looks at her. "You keep," he says. Two words. Complete sentence. Final. Maria cues up "The Look." Lulu grabs a beer.

The title is the structural key. Chapter 09 ends with Lulu's arrival — the door banging open, the armor hitting the floor, "Hey bitches! Didja miss me?" seen from inside the longhouse. Chapter 11 is the same moment reached from the other direction, the event that made the entrance what it was. The instant replay runs backwards from the karaoke to the killing field and back again.

The battle sections are titled after AC/DC songs — "Shoot to Thrill," "Dirty Deeds Done Dirt Cheap," "Big Balls," "Never Was a Quitter" — exactly the songs she performed at karaoke in chapter 09. She didn't perform them. She testified to them. The performance in chapter 09 was documentary evidence of what had just happened in the dark. The spear-in-the-lights moment in Lively ("for a moment she was limned in light, brandishing a spear, and then it was just the mic stand — maybe") is fully earned here. She had just spent three hours with a weapon that weighs half a tonne.

There is a Hervor reading available — she claimed her father's cursed sword from his burial mound, refusing to be limited by what a child should be. But the stronger mantle being assumed here is Thor's. Odin/Forest built the hammer. It could only be lifted by the worthy. Lulu lifts it, takes it into battle, and is told to keep it. Oleksiy — who carried it because Forest gave it to him — looks at the weapon and the girl and says "suits you." Two words. The pantheon continues to assemble; mantles are assumed not by ceremony but by the simple fact of fitness. The weapon recognizes her. That is all the ceremony required.
