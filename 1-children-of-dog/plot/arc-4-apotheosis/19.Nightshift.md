The call comes at 02:17. Antithesis incursion twenty kilometres south-southwest, moving fast through terrain that should have slowed them, headed for homesteads the government has long since stopped defending. Forty-seven people. Sixteen children. Time to evacuate: not enough. Sophia is in the air before she finishes reading the alert.

Oleksiy sets her down on the ideal ridge — good sightlines, clear fields of fire — without discussion. She watches a family load the last of their things into a ute, the man going back for one more bag while his wife gets the children in. They have ninety seconds. She holds the line through three waves: Threes in packs, Fours cutting lanes, a Model Eight hanging back at range directing traffic, testing for weakness, unwilling to commit without finding one. She holds. The last homestead clears. The Eight withdraws. Forty-seven people out, sixteen children safe, no casualties. Autumn tells her she crossed over tonight — not the killing, but being alone out there with no Forest and no backup. Just her and the line.

She steps off the helicopter into pre-dawn light and a man from the first homestead stops mid-stride. "Forest!" he says, his face lighting up. Then he takes in the details — different build, different way of moving — and his expression shifts through embarrassment to understanding to something like awe. "I have cleavage now, too," she tells him. Autumn notes that this is going to spread. "Let it," Sophia says. No broadcast, no announcement. Just the work.

The chapter is the Valkyrie's office hours. Arc 4 is called apotheosis, but apotheosis is not a ceremony — it is the accumulation of nights like this one, repeated until the pattern becomes undeniable. The man saw Forest and then saw Sophia and arrived at awe by the same route. Autumn is explicit: Forest said the same thing she just said — "I am the cavalry." The mantle does not pass at the pyre. It passes at 02:17, again and again, in the dark, with no audience except the families already safe and the AI logging the outcome. The morning is the only announcement required.
